# Use bounded rolling operation and separately proved prospective cutovers

Use rolling synchronization for normal operation: 90 inclusive calendar days
through the Europe/Zurich date, bounded catch-up, whole-account evidence and
coverage publication only after complete live success. Preserve canonical
identities and the inherited manual-entry matching rules. Keep prospective mode
available with independently proved opening captures and full post-cutover replay;
neither mode certifies earlier history or cost basis. Full-history reconstruction
cannot be inferred from successful recent reads.

Opening holdings must agree. Independently verified broker cash may differ from
stale destination cash: DRY_RUN displays the proposed balance without updating it.
Retain exact statement amounts except for a unique supported STOCK execution with
contract size 1 and cent-valued currency: API cash, CSV cash and declared execution
total must each remain within 0.01 of one another and signed quantity times price.
Exact matches take priority; ambiguity and all other categories retain refusal.
This bound accommodates corroborated rounding without accepting generic proximity.

The [mode contract](../prospective-sync.md) owns detailed evidence and rollback.
Independent native, read-only and CSV-transition proof remain required for their
respective acceptance scopes. These decisions authorize no production activation,
financial correction, deletion or relaxation of uncertain-result recovery.
